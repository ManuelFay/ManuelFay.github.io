Hey! I am Manu, a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind working on Gemini Pretraining.

Until I defend in November (hopefully with success), I remain a PhD student interested in LLM and information retrieval research, and curious about (way too) many other things! I had the chance to gain distilled knowledge from my advisor Pierre Colombo at CentraleSupélec and more recently  I worked with Hervé Jégou at Meta FAIR Paris.
My research focuses on practical applications of large language models, with focus on Visual Document Retrieval (ColPali, ViDoRe), LLM pretraining (CroissantLLM, Long Context Modeling at Meta), as well as multimodality, automatic evaluation, model memorization, or confidence estimation and contextualization techniques for neural information retrieval.

My work has been published in top international venues (ICLR, ICML, EMNLP, TMLR, COLM), has been featured in the press ([MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2024/07/25/1095347/a-new-tool-for-copyright-holders-can-show-if-their-work-is-in-ai-training-data/), [Nature Magazine](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-024-02599-9), [Usine Digitale](https://www.usine-digitale.fr/article/croissantllm-des-chercheurs-de-centralesupelec-lancent-un-modele-d-ia-open-source-et-bilingue.N2209348), etc.), gave way to many invited talks (Meta, Amazon, IBM, Naver, LlamaIndex, etc.) and has been listed as a top AI innovation of 2024 ([State of AI](https://www.stateof.ai/), [Tech Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/radar/2024/10/tr_technology_radar_vol_31_en.pdf)). Visual Document Retrieval which I pioneered is now largely used across the industry, both in early stage startups, established large tech companies or government agencies.

My PhD was funded through the CIFRE French program in collaboration with [Illuin Technology](https://www.illuin.tech/en/), where before joining Meta, I held a Staff Research Scientist position, and spent a share of my time advising and accompanying various R&D efforts in the LLM and Vision LLM space.
