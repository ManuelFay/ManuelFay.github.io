### Lectures & guest teaching

During my PhD, I assisted Pierre Colombo with his Natural Language Processing courses at ENSAE and CentraleSupélec. I also lectured on language model pretraining at the Paris GenAI School in 2024 and gave guest lectures on NLP and ML privacy at various institutions.

### Student supervision

I have supervised projects ranging from Tech for Good initiatives with UNESCO and health ML hackathons to longer-term internships and student research projects.

I am always open to new opportunities to share knowledge and learn from others!
